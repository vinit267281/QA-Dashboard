Executive Summary

Twelve leave application scenarios were tested end-to-end to validate that the approval workflow routes to the correct approvers, in the correct sequence, for different leave types and conditions. In all twelve scenarios, the leave request progressed through the expected approval chain, each approver's action was correctly reflected in the system, and the final status matched the corresponding record in Oracle. No workflow discrepancies were identified.

  • Compassionate Leave: 2-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Medical Leave: 4-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Annual Leave (Emergency not selected): 1-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Annual Leave (Emergency selected): 4-level approval chain executed correctly, confirming the Emergency toggle correctly extends the approval chain.
  • Sick Leave (Emergency not selected): 2-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Sick Leave (Emergency selected): 4-level approval chain executed correctly, consistent with the Emergency toggle behavior seen for Annual Leave.
  • Medical Escort Leave: 2-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Examination & Study Leave: 3-level approval chain executed correctly, consistently across leave durations of 1, 2, and 11 days.
  • Unauthorized Leave of Absence: 2-level approval chain executed correctly.
  • Unpaid Medical Escort Leave: 4-level approval chain executed correctly.

Conclusion

All twelve tested leave workflows — Compassionate Leave, Medical Leave, Annual Leave, Sick Leave (both Emergency and non-Emergency conditions), Medical Escort Leave, Examination & Study Leave (across 1, 2, and 11-day durations), Unauthorized Leave of Absence, and Unpaid Medical Escort Leave — routed correctly through their respective approval hierarchies. Each approver's action and the resulting leave status were verified against Oracle with no mismatches. Four UI/data-display issues were identified under Annual Leave (all resolved, including the "00" number format for zero leave counts); none of them blocked or altered the approval outcome. Based on the scenarios covered, the Leave Module's approval workflow logic for these leave types is functioning as intended.

Note: This report covers the leave types and conditions listed above. Workflow mapping for the remaining leave types is in progress separately and will be reported once completed.
